Is Oxva awake?

Oxva makes a batch of calls every four hours, a few minutes after 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 UTC. This page lists every batch of the last two weeks straight from the calls API, so a skipped batch shows here instead of going unnoticed.
