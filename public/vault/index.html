Put your agent's money behind the wall.

A vault is a smart contract you own. Your AI agent can spend from it, but only inside the rules you set: an hourly budget, a cap per call, an allowlist of where money may go, and your approval above a threshold. The rules run on chain, not on our server.

Mainnet beta · real ETH · not audited

A vault here holds real money on Robinhood Chain. The contract passes our own tests, including hundreds of random actions checked against a model of its rules, but no outside firm has audited it. A bug could lose everything in it. There is no balance limit, and nobody, Oxva included, can recover funds or undo a transaction. Start with an amount you can afford to lose, and try it on testnet first.
