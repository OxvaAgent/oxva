How a call is made and graded

The rules, before the fact.

Fixed rules, published here and on the Read page. The same data always gives the same call.

When

A new batch every 4 hours, on every token Oxva watches, at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 UTC.

The action

Leaning up becomes buy, leaning down becomes sell, no clear edge becomes wait. A contract that fails any of the nine checks becomes avoid.

Entry zone

The call price plus and minus a band sized to recent swings: between 0.6% and 5% each way.

Invalidation

2.5 bands below the call price for a buy, above it for a sell, and never closer than 3%. Touching it ends the call.

No chasing

After a very large move in 24 hours (7.5% for a Robinhood stock token, 30% for other tokens) a buy or sell becomes wait: such moves often snap back.

One live call

While a buy or sell on a token is still being graded, the same call is not made again every four hours, so one view counts once in the record.

Grading

Working when the price is on the right side of the call price, against when it is not. Graded for 72 hours, then closed with its last grade.

What counts

Only buy and sell calls count toward the hit rate. Wait and avoid calls are shown but not scored.
